Best Free Tools to Track Your Expenses as a Student

You don't need a paid app to understand where your money is actually going.

6 min read Money Moves India Updated 2026

Before worrying about trading or side income, the more foundational habit is simply knowing where your money already goes. Fortunately, tracking expenses doesn't require paying for anything.

Spreadsheets: the simplest starting point

A basic spreadsheet — even a free Google Sheets template — with columns for date, category, and amount is enough to start noticing patterns in your spending. It takes discipline to keep updated, but it's the most flexible option since you control exactly what it tracks.

Free budgeting apps

Several free apps popular in India let you log expenses manually or, in some cases, read transaction SMS alerts automatically to categorise spending with less manual effort. These trade a bit of privacy and setup time for convenience.

What actually matters more than the tool

Almost any tracking method works if you actually use it consistently — the more common failure point is abandoning a tracker after a week or two, not choosing the "wrong" app. Picking the simplest option you'll realistically stick with beats a feature-heavy app you stop opening.

Worth knowingIf an app asks for full access to read your SMS or bank notifications, check its privacy policy and reviews carefully before granting that access — that's a meaningful amount of trust to hand over for a convenience feature.
