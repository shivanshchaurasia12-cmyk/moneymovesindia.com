UPI Payments Setup Guide for a Student Side Hustle

Getting paid safely and quickly when you're selling online, without exposing your personal bank details.

6 min read Money Moves India Updated 2026

If you're selling anything online as a student — reselling on Meesho, taking custom orders over Instagram, or freelancing — you need a reliable way to actually receive payment. UPI is usually the simplest starting point in India, but there are a few things worth setting up properly from day one.

Personal UPI vs a dedicated payment setup

It's tempting to just share your personal UPI ID (linked to your everyday bank account) with customers. It works, but it mixes your personal and business money together from day one, which makes it hard to track what you actually earned versus spent, and can complicate things later if you ever need to show income records.

A cleaner approach

  • Use a separate bank account for business income, even if it's just a second savings account you already have, rather than your main personal account. This alone makes tracking earnings dramatically easier.
  • Generate a dedicated UPI QR code for that account through your banking app, so customers can scan and pay directly — most major banking apps support this for free.
  • Keep a simple running log of every payment received — even a basic spreadsheet with date, amount, and what it was for saves huge headaches later.

Payment gateways, if you're scaling up

Once orders are frequent enough that manual UPI links are becoming a hassle, tools like Razorpay or Instamojo let you generate proper payment links or checkout pages, and some offer free tiers for small sellers. These typically require basic KYC and, depending on scale, business registration — which loops back to the "registering as a minor" consideration if you're under 18.

Worth knowingNever share your UPI PIN with anyone, including someone claiming to be from a bank, payment app, or "processing a refund." Receiving money never requires entering your PIN — that's only needed when you're sending money out. This scam specifically targets small online sellers.

Refunds and cancellations

Decide your refund policy before you start selling, not after your first dispute. A short, clear policy shared upfront (for example: "no refunds after item ships, replacements only for damaged items with photo proof within 48 hours") protects you from disputes and sets clear expectations for customers.

Keeping records for tax purposes

Even informal side income can have tax implications once it crosses certain thresholds. This is genuinely worth a conversation with a parent or a tax professional as your side income grows — this article isn't tax advice, just a nudge not to ignore it once real money starts moving through your account.
