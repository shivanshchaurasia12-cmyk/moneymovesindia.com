About Money Moves India

₹

Written by a student, for students

Money Moves India is run by a Class 10 student based in Prayagraj, India — not a fund house, not a marketing agency, and not sponsored by any broker or app.

This site started from a simple frustration: most content about trading and side income aimed at Indian students is either overly technical, written for adults with existing capital, or thinly disguised advertising for a specific app or broker. Money Moves India is an attempt to do something more honest — plain-language explanations of how trading and side income actually work, written by someone genuinely learning this material rather than someone selling a course about it.

What this site is

  • Educational articles on trading basics, side income ideas, and common money mistakes
  • Written and researched personally, fact-checked against official sources like SEBI and RBI publications where relevant
  • Updated over time as rules, platforms, and information change

What this site is not

  • Not financial, legal, or tax advice — see our full disclaimer
  • Not sponsored content — we don't get paid to recommend a specific broker, app, or platform
  • Not a signal service or a "guaranteed profit" system — anyone promising that isn't being honest with you

Why it matters that this is written by a student

There's real value in learning material written by someone close to the exact starting point you're at, rather than a professional who forgot what it's like to not know any of this yet. That said, being upfront about who's behind a site is also just good practice — you should always know who's telling you what to do with your money, and why.
