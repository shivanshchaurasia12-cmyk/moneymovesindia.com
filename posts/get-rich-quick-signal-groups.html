Why "Get Rich Quick" Signal Groups Are a Trap

The pattern behind trading signal groups that promise guaranteed profits — and why it never holds up.

6 min read Money Moves India Updated 2026

Trading "signal" groups — often found on Telegram or Instagram — promise to tell you exactly when to buy and sell for guaranteed profits, usually for a fee. The pattern behind these groups is worth understanding before you're tempted to join one.

The core problem with guaranteed signals

No legitimate trading strategy can guarantee profits — markets are inherently uncertain, and anyone claiming otherwise is either misunderstanding risk or being deliberately misleading. Genuine professional traders talk in terms of probabilities and risk management, never certainties.

How these groups typically make their money

Often, the group's real income comes from subscription fees or from steering members toward a specific (sometimes unregulated) broker they earn a commission from — not from the signals actually working consistently. Screenshots of huge wins are usually curated, with losing calls quietly left out.

Why it's especially risky combined with leverage

Following someone else's signal removes your own understanding of why a trade makes sense — so when it goes wrong, you often don't know when to cut losses. Combined with high leverage, that's a common way beginner accounts get wiped out quickly.

Worth knowingSEBI has repeatedly issued warnings about unregistered advisors and signal sellers operating in India. If someone is giving paid investment advice without proper registration, that alone is a red flag worth checking before you pay anything.
